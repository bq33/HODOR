# Team

This folder contains onboarding materials, operational processes, and career development resources for the Prism security and trust & safety team.

## Folders

**onboarding/** contains the new hire onboarding guide and recommended reading list. The onboarding guide walks new team members through the HODOR repo structure, key tools and systems, team norms, and first-week objectives. The reading list covers foundational security and T&S concepts that every team member should be familiar with regardless of their specific role.

**processes/** contains documentation of the team's operational processes including on-call rotation, triage SLAs, vendor review procedures, and cross-functional collaboration workflows. These documents are the reference for "how we do things" on the team.

**career-ladders/** contains the career ladder definitions for security engineering and trust & safety analyst roles. These ladders define the expectations, responsibilities, and competencies at each level and are used in performance reviews and growth conversations.
