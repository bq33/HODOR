# Policies and Enforcement

This folder contains Prism's trust & safety policies, enforcement rubrics, and appeal workflows. It defines what behavior is acceptable on the platform, what happens when users violate those standards, and how users can contest enforcement decisions.

## Folders

**policy/** contains the platform policies that define acceptable and prohibited behavior. These include the acceptable use policy, seller standards, content policies, and anti-fraud policies. Each policy document clearly states what is prohibited, why it is prohibited, and what the consequences are.

**enforcement/** contains the rubrics and decision frameworks that analysts and automated systems use to determine appropriate enforcement actions. The enforcement rubric maps violation types to enforcement actions (warning, restriction, suspension, permanent ban) with escalation criteria for each level. Consistency in enforcement is critical for both fairness and legal defensibility.

**appeals/** contains the appeal workflow documentation, including how users submit appeals, the review process, decision criteria, and response timelines. The appeal process is a critical trust signal for the platform: users who feel they have been treated unfairly and have no recourse become vocal critics and potential legal risks.

## Design Principles

Policies should be clear enough that a reasonable user can understand what is and isn't allowed before they encounter enforcement. Enforcement should be consistent enough that similar violations receive similar consequences regardless of which analyst handles the case. Appeals should be accessible enough that a wrongly enforced user can get a human review without excessive friction.
